| Criterion | Evaluation Focus |
|---|---|
| Mesh Completeness | Whether there are Holes, broken surfaces, non-manifold geometry, and overlaps. |
| Topology Regularity | Polygon distribution and the presence of irregular, excessively thin, or unnecessary faces. |
| Surface Smoothness | Whether there are unwanted bumps, surface noise, or distortions. |

Triangles are acceptable for game assets; all-quad topology is not required.
