# Topology Evaluation Criteria

*Preliminary criteria, subject to revision through testing.*

| Criterion | Evaluation Focus |
|---|---|
| Mesh Completeness | Holes, broken surfaces, non-manifold geometry, and overlaps. |
| Topology Regularity | Polygon distribution and irregular or excessively thin faces. |
| Surface Smoothness | Unwanted bumps, surface noise, and distortions. |

Triangles are acceptable for game assets; all-quad topology is not required.
